Guide for schools

1
Create a request

Select the school, add a clear title, choose the needed date, and list all required items with quantities.

2
Review quotations

Open the comparison page, review supplier totals, delivery days, and item pricing before selecting a preferred supplier.

3
Issue the PO

Upload the official Purchase Order or keep a generated PDF copy for your procurement file.

4
Confirm delivery

Check the delivery note, confirm goods received, and record the Goods Received Note only after physical verification.

5
Track payment

Record payment as Processing while government payment procedures are underway, then mark it Paid once confirmed.

Guide for suppliers

1
Check requests

Review requested goods, quantities, school details, and required dates before quoting.

2
Submit a quotation

Create a quotation header, then enter item prices accurately on the pricing screen.

3
Confirm PO

Once selected, confirm the Purchase Order before preparing goods for delivery.

4
Upload delivery note

Record the delivery note number and date after dispatching goods to the school.

5
Submit invoice

Upload or record the invoice after the school confirms receipt through the GRN.
